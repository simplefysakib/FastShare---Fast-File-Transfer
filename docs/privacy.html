Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Short version

FastShare does not collect personal data. Files go straight from one phone to another (or from a phone to a PC). They are not uploaded to our servers.

What stays on your phone

  • Your device name, which you set yourself in Settings.
  • Your transfer history, which you can clear at any time in Settings.
  • A short event log, kept on the phone. It holds no file names and no file contents. You can share it yourself from Settings or from a problem report.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Unity Ads. Unity may use your advertising ID, as described in Unity's own privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Problem reports

A problem report is only sent when you choose to share it or email it. It contains your app version, phone model, Android version, network type and recent events. It does not contain file names or file contents.

Permissions

FastShare asks for permissions only to find nearby phones, connect to them, save received files, and show notifications while a transfer runs. Location permission is needed by Android for nearby device discovery.

Children

FastShare is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the FastShare GitHub page.
